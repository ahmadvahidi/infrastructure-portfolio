A prototype for automated electronic invoice processing

An MVP for reading structured electronic invoice formats, built with data protection and licensing constraints designed in from the start.

Problem

Electronic invoicing in structured formats is becoming mandatory in Germany. Incoming invoices in these formats still had to be read and entered by hand.

Approach

I built a minimum viable workflow that parses the structured formats, extracts the fields that matter, and prepares them for booking. Before choosing any AI component I worked through two constraints: whether invoice data may legally leave the company under data protection rules, and whether the model provider's licensing allows commercial use.

Solution

A working prototype with a documented evaluation of model providers, including which free tiers are usable commercially and which throughput is realistic for this kind of workload.

Result

A prototype plus a decision document. The compliance analysis turned out to be as valuable as the code, because it is the part that decides whether the project can go live at all.

What I learned

  • For any automation touching invoices or personal data, answer the data protection question before writing code. Retrofitting compliance means rewriting.
  • Free tier does not mean free to use commercially. Read the license, not the pricing page.
