Delivering a developer workstation against a hard deadline

A full development environment, permissions, service access and login scripting, ready for a returning developer's first day.

Problem

A developer was returning full time on a fixed date and needed a completely configured workstation: development tools, permissions, group memberships, access to internal services, and a login script with working database connections. The deadline was not negotiable.

Approach

I split the work into what I could do alone and what required the person themselves. Tools bound to personal accounts and licenses cannot be installed by an administrator in advance, so I scheduled a short joint session for exactly those, on a day the person was already on site.

Solution

Machine prepared, groups and service access configured, login script and database connections tested in advance. The license bound tools were installed together in a scheduled session, and the hardware questions were clarified in the same conversation.

Result

Delivered before the deadline, with the joint session limited to the items that genuinely required the user's presence.

What I learned

  • Separate what needs the user from what does not, and prepare everything else in advance. It turns a full day of interruptions into one short meeting.
  • Schedule the joint session for a day the person is already on site for something else.
