Making the asset database answer a budget question

Configured depreciation with a useful life per asset category, so replacement budget can be forecast instead of guessed.

Problem

An inventory tells you what you own. It does not tell you what you will have to replace next year, which is the question finance actually asks.

Approach

I defined a useful life per asset category and configured depreciation on the financial records so that current book value and end of life date are calculated automatically.

Solution

Depreciation settings per category, applied across an asset base with a documented value in the low millions.

Result

Replacement budget planning is now a report instead of a meeting.

What I learned

  • An asset database becomes valuable at the moment it can answer a financial question, not at the moment it is complete.
  • Useful life per category is a business decision. Get it agreed, do not invent it in the configuration screen.
